# Privacy Policy (EN)

Effective date: June 20, 2026

Last updated: June 20, 2026

leocha (the “Service Provider”) operates the BayBoo mobile application (the “App”). BayBoo is a freemium service for parents, guardians, and authorized family members to manage and share a baby’s growth and care records and, optionally, use personalized AI features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is collected and used, who it is shared with, how long it is retained, and the choices and rights available to users.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, BayBoo may collect the following information.

### 1.1 Account and authentication information

- Name or display name
- Email address
- Profile photo
- Login method (email, Google, or Apple)
- User account identifier
- Language, region, time zone, and App settings

If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, BayBoo receives account information that the relevant provider permits it to receive. BayBoo does not collect your Apple or Google account password.

### 1.2 Baby profiles and care records

- Baby’s name, date of birth or calculated age, sex, and profile photo
- Height, weight, and growth records
- Feeding, solid food, sleep, diaper, bowel movement, play, walk, bath, outing, and other activity records
- Dates and times, amounts, durations, types, and notes entered with those records

This information may include personal information about a child and health- or care-related information. It must be entered by a parent, legal guardian, or another adult with lawful authority.

### 1.3 Family sharing and invitation information

- Family space name
- Members’ names, email addresses, roles, and participation status
- Invitee email addresses and invitation history

When you enable family sharing, the baby profiles and records you select are visible to the people you invite, within the permissions assigned to their roles.

### 1.4 Subscription and purchase information

- Subscription product, purchase status, renewal or expiration status, and the account identifier associated with the transaction

Payment method information, such as a payment card number, is processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play. BayBoo does not directly collect or store that payment method information.

### 1.5 Device, notification, and technical information

- App-specific device identifier
- Device manufacturer, model, operating system, platform, and App version
- Push notification token and notification preferences
- IP address, request time, error information, and security-related technical logs

BayBoo does not collect precise location information from your device.

### 1.6 Feedback and support information

- Feedback type and message
- Optional contact information supplied for a response
- App version, platform, language, region, and time zone needed to handle the request

## 2. How We Collect Information

BayBoo collects information when:

- You enter it during registration, baby setup, activity or growth recording, family invitations, settings, or feedback submission;
- You grant camera, photo library, or push notification permission and use the relevant feature;
- The App automatically generates technical information to maintain a session, manage the active device, secure the service, handle errors, or deliver notifications; or
- Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or an app marketplace provides authentication or subscription status in connection with a service you choose to use.

## 3. How We Use Information

The Service Provider uses collected information to:

- Create accounts, authenticate users, verify identity, and secure accounts;
- Store, display, summarize, analyze, and export baby profiles and care or growth records;
- Share records with family members selected by the user and manage permissions;
- Provide activity pattern analysis, recommended activities, notifications, and personalized App functionality;
- Provide personalized AI guidance and analysis when the user separately permits it;
- Verify Pro subscription status, restore purchases, and manage subscriptions;
- Deliver push notifications and apply notification preferences;
- Respond to feedback and support requests;
- Prevent abuse, address failures, and maintain service availability and security; and
- Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

BayBoo does not sell personal information and does not use it for cross-app targeted advertising or provide it to data brokers.

## 4. Personalized AI Features and Disclosure to OpenAI

BayBoo’s personalized AI features are optional features for Pro users. BayBoo does not send personal information to OpenAI unless the user first gives explicit permission in the App’s separate consent screen.

### 4.1 Information that may be sent to OpenAI

- Baby’s name
- Age calculated from the date of birth
- Sex and the region selected by the user
- Types, times, amounts, and durations of feeding, solid food, sleep, diaper, and other relevant activity records
- Height, weight, and growth measurement dates

BayBoo does not send the account email address, account password, user profile photo, or baby profile photo to OpenAI. Free-form notes are not included in AI requests.

### 4.2 Recipient and purpose

The above information is sent to **OpenAI, LLC**, a third-party AI service provider, solely to generate BayBoo’s personalized parenting tips, developmental guidance, lifestyle rhythm analysis, and growth analysis. BayBoo does not use or provide this information for advertising or data-sale purposes.

### 4.3 Permission, refusal, and withdrawal

- AI data-sharing permission is separate from agreement to a Pro subscription.
- If permission is declined, BayBoo does not send personal information to OpenAI.
- Declining does not prevent use of BayBoo features other than the AI features.
- Permission may be granted or withdrawn at any time under **Settings > AI Data Sharing**.
- Withdrawal stops future transfers to OpenAI. AI results generated before withdrawal and stored in the device cache may remain for a limited period under the App’s cache policy.

The AI consent status, the version of the consent notice, and the time it was changed are stored in the user’s account settings. BayBoo’s server verifies valid permission before processing a request to OpenAI.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

- Account information, baby profiles, activity and growth records, family sharing information, and settings are retained while the user maintains an account.
- Push tokens and active-device information are retained while needed for notifications or device management and are deleted or deactivated following logout, token expiration, device replacement, or account deletion, as applicable.
- Subscription and transaction information may be retained as necessary for subscription administration, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.
- Feedback and support information is retained as needed to resolve the request and address related disputes.
- Security and error logs are retained for a limited period necessary to protect and operate the service.

When a user deletes an account in the App’s settings, BayBoo deletes personal information linked to the account from active systems, except information that must be retained by law. Isolated backup copies may remain until deleted according to a service provider’s backup cycle.

When the applicable retention period ends or the processing purpose has been fulfilled, information is deleted or anonymized using methods designed to prevent recovery.

- Export records in a format supported by the App;
- Change family members and permissions or stop sharing;
- Change push notification and individual notification settings;
- Grant or withdraw AI data-sharing permission; and
- Delete the account.

To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, contact support@bayboo.app. The Service Provider may request reasonable verification before fulfilling a request.

## 9. Children’s Information

BayBoo is intended for parents, guardians, and authorized adults, not for direct use by children. The App may process baby profiles and care or growth records entered by an adult.

Users must have lawful authority to enter or share a child’s information and must obtain parental or legal guardian consent where required by law. A child under 13 must not create an account or directly submit personal information. If you believe a child’s information was provided without proper authority, contact support@bayboo.app. The Service Provider will investigate and take appropriate deletion or restriction measures.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect their account credentials and grant family sharing access only to people they trust.
